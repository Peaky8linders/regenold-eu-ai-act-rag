Remediated: Full statutory enumeration of Article 13(3)(a)-(f) limbs (contact details, intended purpose, limitations, human oversight, hardware/lifetime, logs). Cites Article 13.

Q96 (rg_097) • EASY 2/2 PASS (Was 0/2)

Annex III High-Risk Areas & Healthcare

Remediated: Refusal eliminated. Correctly enumerates all 8 Annex III areas and addresses healthcare under essential public services. Cites Annex III, Article 6.

Q17 (rg_018) • EASY 4/4 PASS (Was 1/4)

Amending Annex III Cumulative Conditions

Former Defect: Missing Article 7 anchor; answer hijacked by canned classification verdict, omitting cumulative condition requirements.

Remediated: States both cumulative conditions under Article 7(1) (Annex III area match AND equivalent/greater risk) plus Article 97 delegated powers. Cites Article 7.1, Article 97.6.

Q74 (rg_075) • HARD 2/2 PASS (Was 0/2)

AI Audio Marking for Artistic Work

Former Defect: Conflated Art. 50(2) provider marking with Art. 50(4) deployer disclosure, erroneously claiming artistic purpose is not a carve-out.

Remediated: Explicitly recognizes Article 50(4) third subparagraph: disclosure for artistic works must not hamper display/enjoyment. Cites Article 50.4, Article 50.2.

Q95 (rg_096) • HARD 2/2 PASS (Was 0/2)

Area vs Use Case Distinction & 8 Areas
