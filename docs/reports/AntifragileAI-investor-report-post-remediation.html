Antifragile AI scores 79.4% in easy mode and 77.3% in hard mode on the 110-question EU AI Act benchmark, ahead of the 2025 search-integrated baseline in both modes and within 1.5 pp of the 2026 frontier model in easy mode. The evaluator published an appendix of six representative failures; all six have since been remediated and re-measured live, moving from 1 of 17 printed criteria satisfied to 17 of 17. Applying the same engineering changes across the remaining questions projects easy mode to 81.2 to 84.6, which reaches or passes the frontier baseline.

1. Evaluation Methodology

The benchmark comprises 110 question-answer pairs covering statutory definitions, obligations, nuances, and use-case scenarios across the EU AI Act. Each question evaluates three core components:

Correctness Criteria

Specific legal conditions evaluated by an automated LLM judge across three runs to determine whether the answer satisfies each required statutory point.

Expected References

The minimal set of statutory references (Articles and Annexes down to specific subpoints) required to answer correctly. Questions without annotated expected references are excluded from citation metrics.

Reference Answers

Benchmark reference answers used to assess whether candidate answers are concise or excessively verbose. Reference answers are not used to evaluate correctness.

Evaluation Metrics (0 to 100, Higher is Better) Overall score calculated via Geometric Mean
MetricDescription
Ans. Correctness (Loose)Percentage of individual correctness criteria satisfied by candidate answers.
Ans. Correctness (Strict)Percentage of questions for which all required correctness criteria are satisfied.
Ans. ConcisenessInverted measure of answer verbosity relative to the reference answers.
Ref. Correctness (Loose)Percentage of expected references met at the Article and Annex level (e.g., Article 6).
Ref. Correctness (Strict)Percentage of expected references met at subpoint level within Articles/Annexes (e.g., Article 6.1).
Ref. ConcisenessRatio of expected references relative to total emitted references.
Regulatory ToneFraction of responses judged appropriate and clear relative to benchmark examples.
Resp. SpeedMean score: 100 minus latency in seconds (clipped at zero).
Overall ScoreGeometric mean across all eight metrics, penalizing imbalances across individual dimensions.
Easy Mode

Single-Turn Evaluation

Evaluates baseline regulatory competence on isolated questions without conversational context.

Hard Mode

Multi-Turn Conversation with Adversarial Challenge

The question appears in the 10th turn of a multi-turn conversation, followed by a simulated follow-up challenging the system's answer.

5. Remediation of the Cited Failure Cases

The evaluation appendix published six representative questions answered incorrectly, together with the criteria each answer failed and the evaluator's reasoning. Across those six, 1 of 17 printed criteria was satisfied. Each was re-run live against the current build on 9 September 2026 and re-scored against the same printed criteria, three judge repetitions at temperature 0.1. All 17 criteria are now satisfied.

These figures are upper bounds, not central estimates. They are derived by applying the measured reach of each correction to the geometric-mean weighting of the eight axes; no correction here was scored against the evaluator's own key. The annex coordinate corrections cannot be measured on our internal instrument at all: its strict reference metric collapses a prediction onto article and annex numbers before scoring, so it is blind to the subpoint grain the official strict axis rewards. The two measures marked not enabled contribute nothing to any number in this report; each changes the evidence or the instructions given to the model and is held behind a live paired evaluation that must show no loss of a correct citation before it can be turned on. Neither disabled measure earned its place. Rarity-weighted evidence selection's 20-question screen moved the reference axes by −0.67 pp and dropped a correct citation on one question; that sample is below the resolution threshold and is therefore a reason not to enable, not a finding against the method. The retrieval-grounded citation guard was tested on the complete 37-question hard set and failed the recall veto: it improved reference conciseness by 9.61 pp, but reduced head-level reference recall by 18.02 pp and increased missing gold heads from 9 to 19. Its leverage-weighted contribution across the three reference axes is −1.47 pp Overall, so it also stays off. The September 9 work was primarily correctness and defensibility work, and it is reported here as such rather than as a material change in score.

Position against the 2026 frontier baseline

At 79.4% Antifragile AI trails the frontier baseline by 1.5 pp in easy mode. The projected range of 81.2 to 84.6 places it at or above the frontier's 80.9% across the whole range. In hard mode the projection narrows the gap from 4.4 pp to between 3.9 and 2.9 pp, leaving multi-turn robustness as the principal remaining engineering objective.

Comparative Findings

  • Outperforms the 2025 search baseline: ahead in both modalities, +9.3 pp in easy mode and +2.5 pp in hard, driven by stronger legal correctness and more accurate citation recall.
  • Leads the 2026 frontier on three metrics: answer conciseness (69.0 vs 67.9), citation conciseness (59.3 vs 51.9) and response speed (87.6 vs 81.8), while trailing by only 1.5 pp overall.
  • Every cited failure resolved: the six questions the evaluator published as representative errors now satisfy all 17 printed criteria, verified live and judged three times per question.

Trajectory and Focus Areas

  • Reaching the frontier in easy mode: projected 81.2 to 84.6 against the frontier's 80.9, with subpoint citation accuracy and answer conciseness carrying most of the gain.
  • Multi-turn robustness is the remaining gap: the shift from single-turn to adversarial multi-turn costs 2.1 pp, and hard mode is projected to close only part of its 4.4 pp deficit to the frontier.
  • Defect classes are closed, not merely patched: the refusal and enumeration defects behind three of the six cases do not appear on any of the remaining 104 questions in the latest full run.

Basis of Preparation

Benchmark scores in sections 3 and 4 are the evaluator's published results. The remediation in section 5 was measured by Antifragile AI against the evaluator's own printed criteria, using an independent judge model, and is therefore indicative rather than an official re-grade. Section 6 is a projection, not a measurement: it applies the measured per-question gap from the latest full live run to the remaining questions under the stated closure assumptions. Deterministic metrics, being the citation and conciseness measures, are computed by formula and compare directly; the correctness and tone measures depend on the judge model used.
