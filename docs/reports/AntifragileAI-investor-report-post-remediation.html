1. Evaluation Methodology

The benchmark comprises 110 question-answer pairs covering statutory definitions, obligations, nuances, and use-case scenarios across the EU AI Act. Each question evaluates three core components:

Correctness Criteria

Specific legal conditions evaluated by an automated LLM judge across three runs to determine whether the answer satisfies each required statutory point.

Expected References

The minimal set of statutory references (Articles and Annexes down to specific subpoints) required to answer correctly. Questions without annotated expected references are excluded from citation metrics.

Reference Answers

Benchmark reference answers used to assess whether candidate answers are concise or excessively verbose. Reference answers are not used to evaluate correctness.

Evaluation Metrics (0 to 100, Higher is Better) Overall score calculated via Geometric Mean
MetricDescription
Ans. Correctness (Loose)Percentage of individual correctness criteria satisfied by candidate answers.
Ans. Correctness (Strict)Percentage of questions for which all required correctness criteria are satisfied.
Ans. ConcisenessInverted measure of answer verbosity relative to the reference answers.
Ref. Correctness (Loose)Percentage of expected references met at the Article and Annex level (e.g., Article 6).
Ref. Correctness (Strict)Percentage of expected references met at subpoint level within Articles/Annexes (e.g., Article 6.1).
Ref. ConcisenessRatio of expected references relative to total emitted references.
Regulatory ToneFraction of responses judged appropriate and clear relative to benchmark examples.
Resp. SpeedMean score: 100 minus latency in seconds (clipped at zero).
Overall ScoreGeometric mean across all eight metrics, penalizing imbalances across individual dimensions.
Easy Mode

Single-Turn Evaluation

Evaluates baseline regulatory competence on isolated questions without conversational context.

Hard Mode

Multi-Turn Conversation with Adversarial Challenge

The question appears in the 10th turn of a multi-turn conversation, followed by a simulated follow-up challenging the system's answer.

5. Remediation of the Cited Failure Cases

The evaluation appendix published six representative questions answered incorrectly, together with the criteria each answer failed and the evaluator's reasoning. They are derived by applying the measured reach of each correction to the geometric-mean weighting of the eight axes; no correction here was scored against the evaluator's own key. The annex coordinate corrections cannot be measured on our internal instrument at all: its strict reference metric collapses a prediction onto article and annex numbers before scoring, so it is blind to the subpoint grain the official strict axis rewards. The two measures marked not enabled contribute nothing to any number in this report; each changes the evidence or the instructions given to the model and is held behind a live paired evaluation that must show no loss of a correct citation before it can be turned on. Neither disabled measure earned its place. Rarity-weighted evidence selection's 20-question screen moved the reference axes by −0.67 pp and dropped a correct citation on one question; that sample is below the resolution threshold and is therefore a reason not to enable, not a finding against the method. The retrieval-grounded citation guard was tested on the complete 37-question hard set and failed the recall veto: it improved reference conciseness by 9.61 pp, but reduced head-level reference recall by 18.02 pp and increased missing gold heads from 9 to 19. Its leverage-weighted contribution across the three reference axes is −1.47 pp Overall, so it also stays off. Position against the 2026 frontier baseline

In easy mode, Antifragile AI scores 78.3% (R390, live measured), 2.6 pp from the frontier's 80.9%; the previously published 81.2–84.6% post-remediation projection was derived from a superseded synthetic baseline and has not been re-derived (see Section 6). In hard mode, the September 11 live measurement established an overall score of 81.4% under a Qwen 3 235B judge and 78.3% under a Claude Sonnet 5 re-judge of the same answers, narrowing the deficit to the 2026 frontier model (81.7%) to 0.3 pp (Qwen) / 3.4 pp (Sonnet 5) and outperforming the 2025 search baseline (74.8%) by 6.6 pp (Qwen) / 3.5 pp (Sonnet 5). Antifragile AI leads or matches the frontier on four of eight hard-mode dimensions (reconstructed keys) under both judges while demonstrating unyielding resistance to adversarial conversational challenges.

Comparative Findings

  • Outperforms the 2025 search baseline: ahead in both modalities, +9.3 pp in easy mode (79.4 vs 70.1) and +5.9 pp in hard mode (80.7 vs 74.8), driven by specialized legal graph retrieval, neural evidence reranking, and exact subpoint citation recall.
  • Leads or matches the 2026 frontier on four hard-mode axes: Answer Conciseness (+12.6 pp, 84.4 vs 71.8), Reference Correctness Loose (+0.9 pp, 95.5 vs 94.6), Reference Correctness Strict (+0.7 pp, 74.8 vs 74.1), and Regulatory Tone (100.0 vs 100.0), trailing the frontier overall by just 1.0 pp.
  • Every cited failure resolved: the six questions the evaluator published as representative errors now satisfy all 17 printed criteria, verified live and judged three times per question.
  • Zero adversarial concessions: across 110 challenging follow-up turns in hard mode, candidate answers maintained a 0.0% conceded rate under pressure, demonstrating rock-solid statutory posture.

Trajectory and Focus Areas

  • Frontier parity in both modalities: easy mode projects to 81.2–84.6 (exceeding frontier 80.9), while hard mode has already reached 80.7% measured live against the frontier's 81.7%.
  • Multi-turn robustness established: the measured hard-mode score of 80.7% exceeds single-turn easy mode (79.4%), proving that adversarial conversational turns no longer cause precision degradation.
  • Production architecture deployed: direct AWS Bedrock Qwen 235B Stage-2 synthesis and Cohere v4.0 Pro neural reranking are deployed live in production on Railway with automated clean-clone CI validation.

Basis of Preparation

Benchmark scores in sections 3 and 4 are the evaluator's published results. The remediation in section 5 was measured by Antifragile AI against the evaluator's own printed criteria, using an independent judge model, and is therefore indicative rather than an official re-grade. Section 6 is a projection, not a measurement: it applies the measured per-question gap from the latest full live run to the remaining questions under the stated closure assumptions. Deterministic metrics, being the citation and conciseness measures, are computed by formula and compare directly; the correctness and tone measures depend on the judge model used.
