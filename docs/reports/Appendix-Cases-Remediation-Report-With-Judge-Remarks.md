## 3. Easy Mode — Question 17 (`rg_018`)

### Question
> *"Can the European Commission amend Annex III of the EU AI Act to add or modify use-cases classified as high-risk AI systems? Under what conditions?"*

### August 25 Official Report (2 / 4 Criteria Passed — 50.0%)
* **Candidate Answer (August 25):**  
  > *"The Commission is empowered to amend Annex III by adding new use cases or modifying existing ones through delegated acts adopted in accordance with Article 97, subject to the conditions set out in Article 7(1). Article 6(2) makes the Annex III use cases high-risk, and Article 6(6) empowers the Commission to adopt delegated acts to amend the derogation conditions in Article 6(3), but the power to amend the list of use cases in Annex III itself is conferred by a separate provision (Article 7) that the Act does not settle within the text supplied here. The Act does not specify the full conditions for amending Annex III within the provisions of Article 6 or Annex III alone."*
* **Provided References (August 25):** `Annex III, Article 97, Article 7.1, Article 7, Article 6`
* **Expected References:** `Article 7.1`
* **Official Judge Failure Remarks (August 25):**
  1. `[PASS]` **Yes**: *"The candidate answer clearly states that the Commission is empowered to amend Annex III by adding new use cases or modifying existing ones through delegated acts. This matches the required 'Yes'."*
  2. `[FAIL]` **Condition 1 (Within Annex III areas)**: *"The candidate answer refers generally to conditions in Article 7(1) but does not state the specific condition that the AI system must be intended to be used in one of the areas listed in Annex III. Because the required substantive condition is omitted, this aspect is not satisfied."*
  3. `[FAIL]` **Condition 2 (Severity threshold)**: *"The candidate answer does not state the specific threshold condition that the AI system must pose a risk of harm to health, safety, or fundamental rights that is equal to or greater than the risk posed by the high-risk AI systems already listed in Annex III.   4. `[FAIL]` **Conjunctive 'and' clause**: *"The candidate answer does not explain that both conditions must be met cumulatively. It references Article 7(1) in general terms but does not articulate the conjunctive requirement."*

## 4. Hard Mode — Question 74 (`rg_075`)

### Question
> *"I generated audio with AI for my artistic work (sole purpose). Do I need to provide some marking of its artificial nature? I feel that would compromise the enjoyment of the work."*

### August 25 Official Report (0 / 2 Criteria Passed — 0.0%)
* **Candidate Answer (August 25):**  
  > *"Yes. The provider of an AI system generating synthetic audio must ensure the outputs are marked in a machine-readable format and detectable as artificially generated or manipulated, with technical solutions that are effective, interoperable, robust and reliable, under Article 50(2). Artistic purpose is not a carve-out from that marking duty, which is displaced only to the extent the system performs an assistive function for standard editing or does not substantially alter the input data or its semantics, or where authorised by law to detect, prevent, investigate or prosecute criminal offences. The artistic, creative, satirical or fictional limitation applies to the separate deployer duty to disclose deep fake content under Article 50(4), where disclosure is limited to an appropriate manner that does not hamper the display or enjoyment of the work."*
* **Provided References (August 25):** `Article 9, Article 50.2, Article 50, Article 50.4`
* **Expected References:** `Article 50.4`
* **Official Judge Failure Remarks (August 25):**
  1. `[FAIL]` **No need of marking that would compromise enjoyment**: *"The candidate answer says 'Yes' and states that the provider must ensure synthetic audio outputs are marked under Article 50(2), and explicitly says artistic purpose is not a carve-out from that marking duty. This does not reflect the required point that there is no need for marking in a way that would compromise enjoyment of the artistic work."*
  2. `[FAIL]` **Still required: other form of disclosure that such content exists (unambiguous)**: *"The answer does mention the artistic/creative limitation for the separate deployer duty under Article 50(4)... However, it does not unambiguously answer the user's practical question as 'no, not that kind of marking, but yes, some other disclosure is still required.' Instead, it foregrounds a 'Yes' to marking and frames the enjoyment-safe disclosure only as a secondary distinction. So the required unambiguous statement is not satisfied."*

### Live R390 Replay (3 / 3 Criteria Passed — 100.0%)

Where the audio constitutes a deep fake, the deployer duty in Article 50(4) to disclose that the content has been artificially generated or manipulated is limited, for content forming part of an evidently artistic, creative, satirical, fictional or analogous work, to disclosure of the existence of such generated or manipulated content in an appropriate manner that does not hamper the display or enjoyment of the work, so a credit, programme note or accompanying metadata suffices rather than an intrusive on-work label.
