The direction matters more than the placing. Between the two graded rounds our gap to the frontier baseline closed by 4.9 points in easy mode and 6.1 in hard, and we moved from losing to the 2025 baseline to beating it. Answer correctness rose 17.6 points on both modes.

The eight axes, and where the distance is

Aggregate scores hide which capability is actually short. Broken out, our profile is lopsided in a useful way: we are close on the axes that measure whether the law is stated correctly, and behind on the two that measure restraint — how much unasked material the answer adds, and how many provisions it cites.

Two axes account for most of the distance, and both measure the same instinct: the system tends to answer the question and then keep going — adding an adjacent provision, a carve-out, a neighbouring obligation. That surplus is penalised twice, once as verbosity and once as over-citation.

It is a tractable class of problem. It is not a knowledge gap, and it is not hallucination.

On the integrity of these figures

Ten of the 110 rows failed to score because the judging transport hit a quota limit mid-run. Our harness detected the partial failure and refused to publish the uncorrected average, which would have understated quality by exactly the error rate. The figures above are computed over the 100 rows that scored.

These are also our instrument, not the evaluator's. The external grader has never published its per-question criteria or expected citation sets, so we grade against the Regulation's own text. We treat our numbers as a proxy and report the third party's as the record.

What is defensible

The benchmark rewards a general-purpose model with a search tool. What a specialist system can hold that such a model cannot is not raw fluency — it is provenance.

Every answer is traceable to statute

Answers are composed from a curated knowledge base and a graph of the Regulation's 113 articles and 13 annexes, then grounded against verbatim provision text. A citation faithfulness of 0.96 is the measurable form of that: the system does not cite what it cannot support.

Every answer is on the record

13,477 question-and-answer pairs are persisted in a tamper-evident hash chain, each entry linked to its predecessor. For a compliance buyer this is the difference between a tool that gives an opinion and one that can evidence what it said, when, and on what basis — which is the posture a regulated deployer must itself be in.

It is fast enough to sit in a workflow

A 10.4-second median, beating a frontier model with search by 5.8 points on the speed axis, is the difference between a system consulted inside a review and one consulted instead of it.

The engineering is measured, not asserted

Changes ship behind a gate that fails a release if it drops a single required citation. That discipline has rejected several of our own promising ideas on the evidence: a reference cap projected to gain a point was withdrawn when a full-corpus test showed it losing required citations, and a prompt change was reverted when measurement showed it was never reaching the model at all.

What is not solved

Three things stand between the current score and the frontier baseline. All three are understood; none is closed.

Open itemCostStatus
Verbosity in short-form answers. Simple lookups grew 49% longer in the latest round while complex ones tightened 14%. −16.0 Root cause identified; remedy fails the citation gate and is being reworked.
Citation granularity. We cite the correct article but not the specific sub-paragraph the grader expects. −10.2 Diagnosed this week: a post-processing step was discarding every sub-paragraph citation the system produced. Fix designed and projected cost-free.
Over-citation. Reference precision 0.74 — the governing provision is found, then accompanied by others. 81% of citation failures are surplus alone, with nothing missing. −6.7 Headroom measured this week: removing exactly the surplus would take citation accuracy from 0.48 to 0.90, above the 0.87 our answers already reach. Our best automatic detector currently recovers a third of that. The remaining distance is detector quality, and it is now the active workstream.

We publish these because the diagnosis is the asset. A team that can say precisely which mechanism costs which point, and decline its own fixes when the evidence does not support them, is a team that can close the gap.
