## Suggestions

- `_ART5_TRIGGER_RE` (`_graph_rag_impl.py`) includes broad stems (`profil\w*`,
  `categoris\w*`). Measured blast radius is 1 of 110 corpus rows (`rg_007`), but
  out-of-corpus behaviour is wider than that number implies. The consequence is a
  *wording* choice, not a different answer path, and it is env-reversible.
- `_HOSPITAL_DEPLOYER_ATTRIBUTION_RE`'s modal alternative
  (`\bhospital\b[^.?]{0,80}\b(?:must|shall|...)`) can span clauses inside one
  sentence. Terminating on `[.?]` prevents crossing sentences, so the residual
  risk is a within-sentence false positive only.
- `_USER_INFORMATION_INTERACT_RE` still fires on
  *"Must an employer be informed when workers interact with AI systems in the
  workplace?"* — a subject that is not a natural person. The answer it returns is
  Article 50(1), which is adjacent but not the worker-notification duty. Left as
  a recorded precision note rather than tightened, because the tightening would
  need its own corpus measurement.
- The definition fix does not close every unresolved-definition row: `rg_066`
  still ships "The Commission shall be the controller of the EU database." for
  "what is the EU database for high-risk AI systems ...". That row has no explicit
  definition phrasing, so it is outside this fix's scope by construction and is
  recorded as a residual, not silently widened.
