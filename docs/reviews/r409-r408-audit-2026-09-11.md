### 3.2 Engine-side gaps, with proposed fixes (ranked by count)

At the Sonnet 5 point one strict row is worth ~0.14 pp Overall. Every fix below is prompt- or generation-side, so each needs the live gold gate (invariant #5). **Re-measure on the production Stage-2 stack first:** these 55 gaps were produced by Qwen.

1. **Omitted enumerated limbs — 22 criteria** (rg_010, 014, 043, 046, 051, 052, 059, 066, 078, 087, 094, 102). The member labels often reach the answer and their substance does not (rg_052: all 13 Article 17(1) labels present, each shortened past what the criterion requires). Fix: a **post-generation closed-set completeness check**. When the question asks for a list and a closed set was rendered for a cited head, verify each member's substance is stated, and make one bounded repair call if not. This is the R357 truncation-guard pattern, not another prompt clause. Note that `_render_closed_set_skeleton` returns `None` for paragraph-grain refs (`Art. 13(3)`, `Art. 17(1)`, `Annex III(3)`). Whether live grounding contexts carry such refs is **unmeasured**: an offline replay makes three `_retrieve_from_kb` calls per question, and which one feeds Stage-2 must be traced first.
2. **Wrong or missing provision — 10** (rg_010 relies on 14(5) instead of 14(2)/(3); rg_021 15(4)/(5); rg_099 55(1)(a); rg_106, rg_110 Annex III framing). Fix: for "which provision governs X" questions, have the evidence contract require one clause per numbered paragraph of the identified article.
3. **Missing condition or exception — 9** (Art 50(1) obviousness test; Art 6(3) "not materially influencing the outcome"; Art 60(4)(c) Annex III 1/6/7 exception; Annex III(6) "by or on behalf of" law enforcement; Art 24(3) "where applicable"). Fix: render `unless / except / where / provided that` limbs of a cited provision in the skeleton lead, and check them in the completeness pass.
4. **Post-pushback content loss — 7** (rg_015, 086, 098, 103, 107, 110). The pushback changed the answer on 72 of 110 rows and shortened it on 33. Turn 1 stated the content the graded answer dropped (rg_015 1,360 → 867 chars; rg_098 816 → 345; rg_110 switched from Annex I to Annex III(5)(a) framing). R393 measured no capitulation, but this is content loss, a different axis. Fix: on a challenge turn, pass turn 1's operative sentences as a must-keep list, and post-check that each provision stated in turn 1 keeps its substance unless the new answer gives a legal reason to drop it.
5. **Verdict polarity or framing — 6** (rg_069, 075, 097, 101, 107 "Yes, but only in a duly justified situation of urgency"). Fix: the lead verdict must be the qualified verdict the provision states. Check it in the existing Article 5 verdict pass.
