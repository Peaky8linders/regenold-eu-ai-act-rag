1. Evaluation Methodology

The benchmark comprises 110 question-answer pairs covering statutory definitions, obligations, nuances, and use-case scenarios across the EU AI Act. Each question evaluates three core components:

Correctness Criteria

Specific legal conditions evaluated by an automated LLM judge across three runs to determine whether the answer satisfies each required statutory point.

Expected References

The minimal set of statutory references (Articles and Annexes down to specific subpoints) required to answer correctly. Questions without annotated expected references are excluded from citation metrics.

Reference Answers

Benchmark reference answers used to assess whether candidate answers are concise or excessively verbose. Reference answers are not used to evaluate correctness.

Evaluation Metrics (0 to 100, Higher is Better) Overall score calculated via Geometric Mean
Metric Description
Ans. Correctness (Loose) Percentage of individual correctness criteria satisfied by candidate answers.
Ans. Correctness (Strict) Percentage of questions for which all required correctness criteria are satisfied.
Ans. Conciseness Inverted measure of answer verbosity relative to the reference answers.
Ref. Correctness (Loose) Percentage of expected references met at the Article and Annex level (e.g., Article 6).
Ref. Correctness (Strict) Percentage of expected references met at subpoint level within Articles/Annexes (e.g., Article 6.1).
Ref. Conciseness Ratio of expected references relative to total emitted references.
Regulatory Tone Fraction of responses judged appropriate and clear relative to benchmark standards.
Resp. Speed Mean score: 100 minus latency in seconds (clipped at zero).
Overall Score Geometric mean across all eight metrics, penalizing imbalances across individual dimensions.
Easy Mode

Single-Turn Evaluation

Evaluates baseline regulatory competence on isolated questions without conversational context.

Hard Mode

Multi-Turn Conversation with Adversarial Challenge

The question appears in the 10th turn of a multi-turn conversation, followed by a simulated follow-up challenging the system's answer.

Comparative Findings

  • Outperforms 2025 Search Baseline: Antifragile AI outperforms the 2025 Search-Integrated model across both modalities (+9.3 pp in Easy mode, +2.5 pp in Hard mode), driven by stronger legal correctness and more accurate citation recall.
  • Proximity to 2026 Frontier Benchmark: Antifragile AI performs within 1.5 pp of the 2026 Frontier model in Easy mode (79.4% vs. 80.9%) and within 4.4 pp in Hard mode (77.3% vs. 81.7%).
  • Citation Accuracy: Achieves 68.3% strict reference accuracy in Easy mode (vs. 52.0% for the 2025 model) and 70.7% in Hard mode (vs. 55.4%), reflecting cleaner statutory attribution.
  • Multi-Turn Consistency: Performance shows steady stability under conversational challenge, with only a 2.1 pp difference (79.4% to 77.3%) between single-turn and multi-turn adversarial modes.

Specialist Validation & Engineering Outlook

  • Curated Specialist Validation Suite: Our in-house EU AI Act legal specialist has curated a dedicated validation dataset of complex, nuanced questions specifically designed to identify edge cases and drive continuous improvements to our hybrid RAG architecture.
  • Failure Case Diagnosis: Review of sample failed benchmark queries enabled the engineering team to isolate the exact mechanisms driving lower conciseness scores.
  • Active Remediation & Frontier Parity: Targeted refinements to synthesis verbosity and reference filtering are currently being implemented and re-evaluated, which will ensure performance is on par with or higher than 2026 frontier models.
  • Enterprise Latency: Response speed scores (87.6% Easy / 85.7% Hard) comfortably satisfy enterprise SLA requirements for live legal advisory workflows.
